# Diversity Hiring Guidelines

Every open role must have at least one candidate from an underrepresented group
included in the final interview shortlist before an offer decision is made,
provided a qualified candidate from that pool applied. Recruiters are expected to
actively source from diversity-focused job boards and employee referral networks
for roles that have historically skewed toward one demographic group, such as
Software Engineer and Product Manager positions. Diversity hiring targets are
tracked at the quarterly level across all categories and are reviewed by HR
leadership, not used as a hard quota that overrides merit-based selection for any
single role. Interview panels for senior roles should include at least one panelist
from a different gender or background than the hiring manager where feasible, to
reduce single-perspective bias. All diversity-related candidate data is collected on
a voluntary, self-identified basis and is never shared with the hiring manager
during the evaluation stage.
