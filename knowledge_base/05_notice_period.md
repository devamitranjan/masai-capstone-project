# Notice Period Policy

The standard notice period is 30 days for individual contributor roles such as
Software Engineer, Data Analyst, and Sales Associate, and 60 days for managerial
roles such as Product Manager and HR Executive. A new hire may request notice period
buyout assistance, where the company reimburses a portion of the shortfall amount
their previous employer deducts, subject to manager and HR approval before the offer
is finalized. Candidates serving a notice period longer than 90 days at their current
employer are generally not considered unless the hiring manager grants an explicit
exception for a critical role. Any change to an agreed joining date caused by notice
period negotiation must be communicated to the recruiter at least five working days
in advance. Unused notice period days cannot be carried forward or encashed after
joining the new role.
