# Referral Bonus Policy

Employees who refer a candidate that is eventually hired are eligible for a referral
bonus, paid out in two parts: 40% on the referred candidate's date of joining and the
remaining 60% after the candidate completes their probation period. Referral bonus
amounts vary by role seniority, with senior or hard-to-fill roles such as Product
Manager carrying a higher bonus than entry-level Sales Associate or HR Executive
roles. An employee cannot refer a candidate for a role they are directly responsible
for interviewing or hiring into, to avoid a conflict of interest. Referral bonuses are
forfeited if the referred candidate resigns or is terminated for cause before
completing 90 days of employment. Only one employee can claim the referral bonus per
candidate, determined by whoever's referral was logged first in the system.
