# Job Application Eligibility Criteria

Candidates applying through Naukri.com must hold the minimum educational qualification
listed on the job posting and meet any stated minimum years of relevant experience
before their application is considered complete. Applicants who have been rejected for
the same role within the last 90 days are not eligible to reapply for that exact
position, though they may apply to other open roles at the same company. Work
authorization for the job location is mandatory at the time of application; candidates
requiring future sponsorship must disclose this in their profile. Internal employees
applying to a new role must have completed at least six months in their current role
and must inform their current manager before applying. Applications with an
incomplete profile, missing resume, or missing contact details are auto-flagged and
excluded from recruiter review until completed.
