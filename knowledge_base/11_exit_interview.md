# Exit Interview Process

Every employee who resigns or is separated, voluntarily or involuntarily, is
offered an exit interview conducted by HR rather than their direct manager, to
encourage candid feedback. The exit interview is scheduled within the first week of
the employee's notice period and covers reasons for leaving, overall experience,
and any unresolved concerns about their role or team. Participation in the exit
interview is voluntary, and declining to participate does not affect the
employee's final settlement, relieving letter, or future eligibility for rehire.
Feedback collected during exit interviews is aggregated and anonymized before being
shared with department leadership, unless the employee explicitly consents to
attributed feedback being shared. Employees exiting during or immediately after
probation follow the same exit interview process as confirmed employees.
