# Internal Transfer Eligibility

Employees become eligible to apply for an internal transfer after completing 12
months in their current role, with an exception for employees whose team is being
restructured or shut down. An internal transfer application requires the employee's
current manager to acknowledge the request, though the manager's approval is not
required to proceed to an interview with the receiving team. Employees under a
formal performance improvement plan are not eligible for internal transfer until the
plan concludes with a satisfactory outcome. Compensation changes resulting from an
internal transfer follow the same approved band logic as an external offer for that
role and level, rather than simply carrying forward the employee's existing salary.
An approved internal transfer typically has a release date of two to four weeks from
approval, coordinated between the current and receiving managers.
