# Probation Period Policy

All new hires, regardless of category, serve a probation period of 90 days from
their date of joining, during which either party may end the employment with
seven days' written notice instead of the standard notice period. A probation
review is conducted by the hiring manager around the 75-day mark, covering
performance against the role's expectations and overall fit. Probation may be
extended by up to 30 additional days if the review is inconclusive, but it cannot
be extended more than once for the same employee. Confirmation of employment after
probation is communicated in writing and triggers eligibility for benefits that are
not available during the probation window, such as the referral bonus payout tied
to probation completion. An employee who is not confirmed at the end of an extended
probation period is exited following the standard involuntary-exit process.
