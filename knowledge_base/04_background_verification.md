# Background Verification Process

Background verification begins only after a candidate has accepted a written offer,
and covers employment history, education credentials, and, where legally permitted,
criminal record checks. The verification vendor typically completes checks within
10 business days, though checks involving international employment history can take
up to three weeks. Any discrepancy found between a candidate's stated employment
history and the verification vendor's findings is first shared with the candidate for
clarification before any decision is made. A confirmed discrepancy in education
credentials or employment dates can result in withdrawal of the offer, while minor
clerical mismatches (such as a former employer's name change) do not affect the
offer. Candidates are notified of their verification status only after the process
fully completes; partial or in-progress results are not shared externally.
