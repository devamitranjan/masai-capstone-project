# Offer Negotiation Policy

After an offer is extended, the candidate has seven calendar days to accept, decline,
or request a negotiation before the offer automatically expires. Salary negotiation
requests must be routed through the assigned recruiter rather than directly to the
hiring manager, and any revised offer must stay within the approved budget band for
that role and level. A candidate may request one round of salary negotiation and one
round of joining-date negotiation per offer; additional requests require director-level
sign-off. Counter-offers that exceed the approved band by more than 10% are escalated
to HR leadership for a case-by-case decision rather than being approved by the
recruiter directly. Verbal commitments made during interviews are not binding; only
the written offer letter reflects the final negotiated terms.
