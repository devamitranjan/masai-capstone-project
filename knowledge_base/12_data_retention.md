# Applicant Data Retention Policy

Applicant data, including resumes, contact details, and interview notes, is
retained for 24 months from the date of the last activity on the application, after
which it is automatically purged from active systems. Candidates who are hired have
their application data migrated into the employee record system and are no longer
subject to the applicant retention timeline. A candidate may request earlier
deletion of their applicant data at any time by submitting a data deletion request
through Naukri.com, which must be processed within 30 days. Background verification
results and offer letters are retained for a longer statutory period of 7 years for
hired employees, independent of the general applicant data retention timeline.
Aggregated, de-identified application statistics used for hiring analytics are
exempt from the deletion timeline since they no longer reference an individual
candidate.
