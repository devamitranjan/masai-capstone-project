# Interview Scheduling Process

Once a recruiter shortlists a candidate, the scheduling team sends an interview slot
proposal within three business days, offering at least two alternative time windows.
Candidates must confirm or request a reschedule within 48 hours of receiving the
proposal, or the slot is released back to the scheduling pool. Technical interviews
for Software Engineer and Data Analyst roles are conducted in two rounds: a screening
round and a panel round, while Product Manager and HR Executive roles typically have a
single panel round followed by a final leadership discussion. All interview rounds are
conducted over video call unless the candidate and hiring manager mutually agree to an
in-person round at a company office. A candidate may reschedule an interview at most
twice per application; a third reschedule request requires recruiter approval.
