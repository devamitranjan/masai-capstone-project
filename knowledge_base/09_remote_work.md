# Remote Work Eligibility

Remote work eligibility depends on the role category: Software Engineer and Data
Analyst roles are generally eligible for fully remote or hybrid arrangements, while
Sales Associate roles require regular field or client-site presence and are only
eligible for partial remote work. Product Manager and HR Executive roles are
evaluated case-by-case based on how much of the role depends on in-person
stakeholder collaboration. A new hire requesting fully remote work must have this
agreed in writing before their offer is finalized, since retroactive remote
approval after joining requires additional manager and HR sign-off. Employees
approved for remote work must remain reachable during the core overlap hours
defined by their team, regardless of time zone. Remote work approval can be revoked
if a role's responsibilities change significantly, with 30 days' notice given to
transition back to an in-office or hybrid arrangement.
